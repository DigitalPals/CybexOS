01 / YOUR SETUP

Make it yours.

One password for your disk and account. Unlock once at startup and arrive at your desktop.

Use at least 12 characters. Your chosen layout will also be used at the boot unlock screen.

For your security, sudo asks for your account password by default. Enable this only if you want passwordless administrator commands.

More options

Installs without an account. At first start, the new owner chooses a language, keyboard, account, password and timezone.

03 / REVIEW AND INSTALL

Ready when you are.

Planned disk changes

    Your disk and account passwords start out the same. Changing your account password later does not change the disk password. Your app keyring stays encrypted and unlocks with the disk password when they match. If automatic unlock is unavailable, an app will ask for the keyring password.
